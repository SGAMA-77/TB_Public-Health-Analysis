# TB Public Health Analysis: South Africa

## Project Overview
This project delves into the nature and status of Tuberculosis (TB) in South Africa, analyzing public health indicators to extract key insights, generate informative visualizations, and summarize critical findings. The analysis integrates qualitative data from a public health report with quantitative data from a comprehensive dataset.

## Dataset
The primary dataset used for this analysis is `tuberculosis_indicators_zaf.csv`, obtained from [https://data.humdata.org/dataset/who-data-for-zaf](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/who-data-for-zaf). This dataset contains various tuberculosis indicators specific to South Africa over several years.

## Key Findings

*   **TB Incidence Decline**: Total TB incidence peaked in 2007 at 1,270 cases per 100,000 population, subsequently declining to 389 cases per 100,000 by 2024. This significant reduction is closely linked to the stabilization of the HIV epidemic and the scale-up of Antiretroviral Therapy (ART).
*   **High Integration of TB and HIV Services**: South Africa has achieved near-universal HIV status awareness among TB patients, rising from approximately 10% in 2004 to nearly 97% by 2015. ART coverage for co-infected patients currently stands at around 97%, demonstrating dramatic success in integrated care.
*   **Improved Treatment Efficacy**: Treatment success rates for new TB cases peaked at 82% in 2016 and have generally remained high. For Multidrug-Resistant TB (MDR-TB), success rates showed significant improvement, increasing from 40% in 2010 to 63% in 2022.
*   **Decreased MDR-TB Burden**: Confirmed MDR-TB cases peaked in 2013 with over 26,000 cases, followed by a noticeable decline to approximately 6,200 cases by 2024, reflecting effective control measures and overall reduction in TB transmission.

## Visualizations
Several visualizations were generated to support and illustrate the key findings:

*   **`tb_incidence_trends.png`**: Trends in overall and HIV-associated TB incidence over time.
*   **`tb_hiv_integration.png`**: Integration of TB and HIV services, showing HIV status awareness and ART coverage among TB patients.
*   **`tb_treatment_success.png`**: Treatment success rates for new TB cases and MDR-TB.
*   **`mdr_tb_burden.png`**: Confirmed MDR-TB cases over time.

These visualizations corroborate the insights derived from the 'TB_Public_Health_Report_South_Africa.docx', providing a clear graphical representation of the public health landscape.
